## Comparison

The Ricci-flow literature supplies a different but structurally comparable pattern:

singular regime → surgery → continued flow.

Perelman explicitly constructs Ricci flow with surgeries on three-manifolds. The surgery procedure is part of the mathematical construction used to continue the flow beyond singular behavior.

The comparison therefore has three genuine structural axes:

1. **continuation obstruction**
2. **transition to a new regime**
3. **continuation after transition**

But the analogy stops there unless new mathematics is supplied.

### The decisive difference

In Chapter 3, P4 → P5 is a **scale/localization transition**.

In Ricci flow, surgery is a **specific geometric modification of a manifold/metric near a singular region**.

The Chapter 3 text does not currently define an operator that performs the Ricci-type operation. It also does not define curvature blow-up, a singular set, neck regions, cutting, capping, or the invariants that would have to survive the transition.

Therefore the strong hypothesis

> P4 → P5 = Ricci surgery

is **not established**.

The weaker hypothesis

> Fr → transition → continuation is structurally comparable to singularity → surgery → continuation

has substantial source-grounded support and remains a legitimate research hypothesis.

The next question is no longer:

> "Does P4 → P5 look like surgery?"

> **What mathematical operator is P4 → P5?**

If that operator can be independently defined, one can then ask whether it has a Ricci-flow interpretation.

That reverses the dangerous direction of reasoning. We do not define the ontodynamic operator by copying Ricci surgery; we first derive the operator from the ontology and only afterwards test correspondence.

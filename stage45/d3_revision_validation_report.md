# D3 Revision Validation Report

## Result

PASS — research-process validation.

## Verified

1. Exhaustion of external orthogonal continuation is explicit and source-grounded.
2. Exhaustion is represented as a condition on an admissible continuation set.
3. Frustration is formalized as continuation requirement plus failure of the previous continuation method.
4. P4 is explicitly prevented from becoming a fourth spatial basis vector.
5. Orientation reversal is attached to a regime transition rather than treated as a free reflection.
6. The square of the processual transition remains undefined until its carrier and operation are established.
7. The numerical rank model is labeled as a proposal rather than a source fact.
8. Clifford/Dirac identification remains an open research question.
9. Canonical promotion remains false.

## Limitation

`D_ext(P)` is introduced by this revision. The source motivates the object but does not name or axiomatize it. It therefore remains a formalization, not an additional source claim.

## Research consequence

D3 is revised from:

`fixed vector space -> bilinear form -> reversal`

to:

`available continuation -> exhaustion -> frustration -> regime transition -> orientation reversal -> possible state-space/algebraic structure`.

The next mathematical task is to define a continuation space rigorously enough to test whether the exhaustion transition can be represented algebraically.

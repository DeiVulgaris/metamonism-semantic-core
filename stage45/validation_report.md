## Result

**INCONCLUSIVE / UNRESOLVED**

1. P1 is introduced as a minimal actualization of difference.
2. The present cannot settle into absolute identity.
3. Actualization continuously resolves into a subsequent, non-identical act.
4. Orthogonal resolution is described as the mechanism preserving difference without collapse.
5. The text explicitly refers to frustration of self-identity as the problem resolved by this mechanism.

However, the Ricci-flow side of the proposed correspondence is absent from this corpus. There is no Ricci equation, curvature quantity, singularity criterion, surgery construction, or continuation theorem.

The bidirectional correspondence remains **UNRESOLVED**.

## Important distinction

This corpus does not itself establish the full frustration definition used in the earlier Chapter 3. It only references that previous derivation. Therefore this test must not silently import the complete Chapter 3 machinery and label it as independently present here.

Likewise, the text places physical correspondence in a separate hypothesis layer and explicitly says that mathematical formalization must come later. The present test respects that boundary.

## Research conclusion

The corpus **supports the research question**, not the proposed mathematical identification.

The hypothesis can therefore be retained as:

- epistemic status: `THEORETICAL_HYPOTHESIS`
- relation: `STRUCTURAL_CORRESPONDENCE`
- frontier state: `UNRESOLVED`
- canonical promotion: `false`

# D_ext Transition Operator — Validation Report

## Result

PASS — research-process validation.

## Established at the current formal level

- ordinary continuation is represented by admissible morphisms `Hom_R(S_i,S_j)`;
- exhaustion is the absence of all admissible outgoing arrows under the current regime;
- the frustration boundary is the domain of the regime-changing transition;
- `J_R` maps the boundary of one regime into a new regime;
- P4 is therefore represented as the result of a boundary transition, not as another external continuation direction.

## Not established

`J_R` is not yet a linear map, matrix, reflection, Clifford generator, or endomorphism. `J_R²` is undefined. A common carrier `W` is only a proposal.

## Mathematical fork

A future formalization must test whether the old and new regimes can be represented on one carrier without collapsing their distinction. If yes, an operator algebra may be constructed. If not, a multi-regime transition/category structure may be the more faithful mathematical object.

No Clifford or Dirac relation is assumed or derived at this stage.

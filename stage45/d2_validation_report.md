PASS at the research-process level.

D2 successfully identifies a source-faithful composition mechanism: compatible sequential transition composition.

## Established

- P_i remain processual states.
- T_i represent transitions between successive processual states.
- Compatible transitions compose.
- Composition is associative where represented as ordinary map/path composition.
- R is kept distinct from T_i and acts on orientation data n.
- R(n) = -n is preserved from the source.
- R² remains unresolved.

## Not established

- A common operator carrier.
- A scalar/additive structure.
- A bilinear form.
- Clifford multiplication.
- Any Dirac-matrix representation.

## Interpretation

The natural mathematical object produced by the source at D2 is path-like transition composition rather than an algebra.

This is a positive structural finding because it prevents a premature identification of processual succession with algebraic multiplication.

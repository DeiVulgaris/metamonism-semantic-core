# D3 Validation Report

## Outcome

PASS at the research-process level.

D3 yields a conservative bilinear formalization without importing a metric signature or Clifford algebra.

## Result

Working carrier:

V = span{e1,e2,e3}

Working bilinear form:

q = diag(a1,a2,a3)

with unresolved coefficients and signature.

Source-grounded orthogonality is represented by:

q(e_i,e_j)=0 for i != j.

## Important result

For the explicitly linear reversal R(n)=-n:

q(Rn,Rn)=q(-n,-n)=q(n,n).

Therefore orientation reversal does not by itself generate a negative metric component.

Likewise, if R is explicitly extended linearly on the defined subspace:

R^2=I.

This is a consequence of the chosen mathematical formalization, not a claim that Chapter 3 independently defines a global involution.

## Not established

- Euclidean normalization.
- Lorentzian signature.
- A fourth metric dimension.
- Clifford multiplication.
- Dirac matrices.
- Spinor structure.

## Research consequence

Any Lorentzian signature or non-involutive orientation algebra must come from an additional structural mechanism. It cannot be obtained simply by relabeling +n -> -n as a metric sign.

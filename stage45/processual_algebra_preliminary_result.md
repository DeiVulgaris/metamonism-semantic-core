# Preliminary D1-D5 Result

## 1. First mathematical consequence

A literal identification

P1 <-> e1
P2 <-> e2
P3 <-> e3
P4 <-> e4

is not currently justified by Chapter 3.

The source says that after the three external orthogonal directions are exhausted, continuation occurs by reversal of the normal:

+n -> -n.

It explicitly says that P4 is not a fourth spatial dimension.

Therefore a more faithful first formalization is:

V = R^3

with three independent directional basis elements n1, n2, n3, together with an orientation-reversal operator R acting on the relevant normal:

R(n) = -n.

This is a working mathematical representation, not a claim that the source itself defines V = R^3.

## 2. Processual rather than state-generator representation

The candidate algebraic objects are therefore more naturally:

- directional objects associated with P1, P2, P3;
- transition operators between processual states;
- an orientation operator R associated with +n -> -n.

This changes the research question.

Instead of:

"Do four processual stages form four gamma generators?"

the stronger and cleaner question is:

"Does the algebra of processual directional/transition operators contain a Clifford substructure?"

That question preserves the source distinction between states and operations.

## 3. D1 candidate

Working carrier:

V = R^3

with an orthogonality relation represented by a symmetric bilinear form q.

At this stage q is not assigned a signature by analogy with Minkowski spacetime.

D1 status: PROPOSED.

Reason: this is a mathematically minimal carrier compatible with the source's explicit statement that P4 is not a fourth spatial dimension.

## 4. D2 candidate

Introduce transition operators:

T1, T2, T3

for the processual realization of the first three directional unfoldings, and R for orientation reversal.

No multiplication law is assumed yet.

Composition such as R T_i or T_i T_j is a research object whose meaning must be defined.

D2 status: UNRESOLVED.

## 5. D3 candidate

The source establishes orthogonality as a structural relation, but does not provide a metric tensor or signature.

Therefore q remains symbolic:

q : V x V -> F.

No identification with Euclidean or Lorentzian metric is made.

D3 status: UNRESOLVED.

## 6. D4 candidate

If an operator algebra A can be defined, test its symmetric product:

{A,B} = AB + BA.

The question is whether some independently identified generators satisfy:

{E_mu,E_nu} = 2 g_mu_nu I.

At present there is no source-grounded derivation of E_mu, the product, g_mu_nu, or I.

D4 status: BLOCKED until D2-D3 are solved.

## 7. D5 candidate

The Clifford condition is therefore a test, not a starting axiom.

D5 status: BLOCKED.

No Clifford algebra has yet been derived.

## 8. Orientation reversal test

The source gives:

R(n) = -n.

This alone does not determine:

R^2 = +I
or
R^2 = -I.

Indeed, in an ordinary vector-space reflection, R^2 may equal I, while other algebraic structures can assign different relations.

The processual model must decide which, if any, relation is warranted.

This is a particularly important test because choosing R^2 = -I merely to obtain spinorial behaviour would be target-theory import.

## 9. Preliminary research result

The first formalization does not confirm the original four-generator Dirac hypothesis.

It produces a more precise research program:

processual states
    ->
directional/transition operators
    ->
operator algebra
    ->
test for Clifford substructure
    ->
representation problem.

Current status:

- D3: UNRESOLVED

- D5: BLOCKED
- Clifford algebra: NOT DERIVED
- Dirac matrices: NOT DERIVED

- Dirac equation: NOT DERIVED

## 10. What would count as a real breakthrough

A meaningful positive result would not be "we can label four things gamma".

It would be an independently derived algebra in which:

1. the carrier follows from the processual model;
2. the composition law follows from processual transitions;
3. the bilinear form follows from independently justified structure;
4. an anticommutator follows from the product;
5. the resulting relations match a Clifford algebra without being stipulated;
6. only then is a matrix representation constructed.

That would constitute a genuine mathematical structural correspondence.

Even then, physical interpretation would remain a separate question.

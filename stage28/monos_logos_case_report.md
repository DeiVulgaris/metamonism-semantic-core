# Stage 28 — Semantic Case Study: Monos / Logos

## Question

What is the relation between Monos and Logos, and what does the corpus actually establish about their correspondence?

## Monos

Monos is registered as continuous, processual differentiation. It is non-symbolic and is not treated as a static substrate.

## Logos

Logos is registered as the epistemic space of models, theories, and fixed representations. Stable relations and discrete entities exist through fix operations.

## Relation

The corpus registers a **structural correspondence** between Monos and Logos.

This is stronger than merely saying that the terms are associated, but weaker than a formal mathematical isomorphism. The semantic core therefore preserves:

```
structurally_corresponds_to = SOURCE
isomorphic_to = UNRESOLVED
```

## Cross-case result

**STRUCTURAL_CORRESPONDENCE**

The case does not establish identity, one-to-one representation, or mathematical isomorphism.

The important semantic distinction is:

> Correspondence between structural roles does not erase categorical difference between the domains.

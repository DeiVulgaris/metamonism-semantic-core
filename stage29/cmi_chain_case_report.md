# Stage 29 — CMI vs CMI Chain

## Purpose

This case tests a relation that cannot safely be represented as identity,
equivalence, or generic structural correspondence: **extension**.

The registered semantic core contains both:

- `mm:core.prc.cmi` — the foundational CMI triad.
- `mm:core.prc.cmi_chain` — an explicitly extended chain.
- `mm:rel.extends` — the registered relation from CMI Chain to CMI.

## Source-grounded distinction

CMI provides the triadic process:

> Prohibition / Conflict → Momentum / Moment-Impulse → Force

CMI Chain retains that segment and continues:

> Prohibition → Momentum → Force → Energy → Dissipation → Arrow of Time

Therefore the relation is not one of identity. The chain contains the CMI structure while adding semantically registered stages.

## Benchmark result

**EXTENSION**

This result means:

1. the target semantic structure is retained;
2. additional semantic content is introduced;
3. the source explicitly registers the extension relation;
4. identity and equivalent formulation are not thereby established.

## Why a separate result class is needed

Stage 27 defined comparison results including `EQUIVALENT_FORMULATION`
and `STRUCTURAL_CORRESPONDENCE`, but neither captures the present case precisely.

Calling the chain merely a structural correspondence loses the directional
fact that one formulation extends another.

Calling it equivalent is incorrect because the extended chain contains
additional registered process stages.

Thus `EXTENSION` is added to the benchmark result vocabulary as a
comparison outcome. It is a benchmark classification, not a new semantic
claim about the theory.

## Forbidden collapse

The following inferences are rejected:

- CMI = CMI Chain.
- CMI Chain is only a restatement of CMI.
- Energy, dissipation, and arrow of time are optional labels with no semantic effect.
- The extension relation proves a stronger physical or mathematical identity.

## Relation to existing derivations

The registered derivation `mm:drv.005` uses the CMI Chain and derives
dissipation as a chain consequence. This does not retroactively make CMI
and CMI Chain identical; it illustrates why preserving the extension is
semantically important.

## Conclusion

The benchmark now distinguishes three different comparison outcomes across
the implemented cases:

- Dissipation across A/B/C → `PARTIAL`
- Monos / Logos → `STRUCTURAL_CORRESPONDENCE`
- CMI / CMI Chain → `EXTENSION`

This increases discrimination without collapsing distinct source meanings.

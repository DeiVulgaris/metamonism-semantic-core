# Stage 17 — Formalization Specification

Stage 17 translates selected registered claims into explicit formal constraints, predicates, transition schemas, protocol invariants, epistemic boundaries, and model mappings.

Formalization is representational. It does not upgrade the epistemic status of the underlying claim.

Status preservation:
- SOURCE remains SOURCE.
- RESEARCH_HYPOTHESIS remains RESEARCH_HYPOTHESIS.
- PROPOSED remains PROPOSED.
- DRAFT_HYPOTHESIS remains DRAFT_HYPOTHESIS.
- MODEL_SPECIFIC remains MODEL_SPECIFIC.

Notation:
- `!=` expresses explicit distinction/non-identity.
- `->` expresses a source-supported implication or transition schema.
- `:=` expresses definitional representation.
- `~` expresses model-level correspondence, not mathematical equality.

No unstated mathematical law is introduced. No formalization proves its underlying claim.

Stage 17 does not merge A/B/C operators, create new ontological entities, elevate models to CORE, or replace external scientific formalisms.

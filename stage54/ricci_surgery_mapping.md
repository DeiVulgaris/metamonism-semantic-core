## A. Frustration and singularity

Result: STRUCTURAL_CORRESPONDENCE; mathematical identification UNRESOLVED.

Common pattern: continuation is required, the current regime develops an obstruction, and a special transition is needed.

Difference: frustration is a processual condition defined by necessary continuation plus exhaustion of the current mode. A Ricci singularity is a geometric/analytic breakdown associated with singular curvature behavior.

Therefore frustration is not a Ricci singularity by definition.

## B. Orthogonal Resolution and surgery

Result: STRUCTURAL_CORRESPONDENCE; mathematical identification UNRESOLVED.

Both occur at a boundary where ordinary continuation in the current form is inadequate, and both yield a state from which continuation may proceed.

Difference: Orthogonal Resolution is currently a logically independent continuation channel. Ricci surgery is a concrete geometric/topological operation performed in controlled high-curvature regions.

Therefore Orthogonal Resolution is not Ricci surgery by definition.

## C. Continuation and post-surgery flow

Result: PARTIAL STRUCTURAL_CORRESPONDENCE.

The common sequence is resolution event followed by subsequent continuation. The state carrier and evolution law remain different.

## D. unfold and surgery

Result: PARTIAL.

Both contain controlled rupture/modification, but unfold specifically invalidates fixation/closure and restores differentiation, whereas Ricci surgery modifies a Riemannian 3-manifold and its metric near singular regions.

## E. Dissipation and Ricci flow

Result: UNRESOLVED.

Continuous geometric evolution and smoothing do not by themselves establish that Ricci flow is the mathematical realization of Meta-Monist dissipation.

## F. Fix and canonicalization

Result: UNRESOLVED.

Ricci flow may produce canonical geometric regimes under suitable hypotheses, but this is not enough to identify it with local representational fixation.

## Critical orthogonality test

Meta-Monism requires an orthogonal continuation because the current differentiation mode is exhausted.

Ricci surgery uses specific geometric structures such as neck regions and 2-sphere cuts. The word orthogonal does not identify those structures.

So the hypothesis is:

    Orthogonal Resolution ↔ Ricci surgery

as structural correspondence only, pending a mathematical state map and surgery construction.

Mathematical reference: Morgan & Tian, Completion of the proof of the Geometrization Conjecture.

Ricci flow is given by:

    ∂g(t)/∂t = -2 Ric(g(t)).

The reference describes Ricci flow with surgery as an evolution that is smooth between discrete surgery times. At surgery times controlled changes of the manifold and metric occur. Surgery cuts away neighborhoods of developing singularities and replaces them with geometrically controlled regions, allowing continuation of the flow.

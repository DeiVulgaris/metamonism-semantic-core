# Stage 20 — Controlled Semantic Expansion

## Purpose

Stage 20 materializes already-registered source semantics into an explicit controlled view.

It does **not** introduce a new ontological layer and does not reinterpret A/B/C.

## Admission rule

A record may enter the Stage 20 materialized view only when:
1. its source registration already exists;
2. its scoped mm:* ID is registered globally;
3. its epistemic status is preserved;
4. its provenance is available at the source-registration level;
5. it does not require a new cross-inventory identity relation.

## Explicitly excluded

- no new ontology;
- no A/B/C operator merging;
- no MODEL → CORE promotion;
- no hypothesis → SOURCE promotion;
- no sameAs / identical_to / implements / proves;
- no inference from missing relations;
- no reconstruction of source claims beyond their registered form.

## Materialization layers

- entities
- claims
- relations
- formalizations
- provenance
- cross-source bridge X1-X21

The materialized view is derived data. The registries remain authoritative.
